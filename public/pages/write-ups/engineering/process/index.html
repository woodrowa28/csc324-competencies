Process

What software engineering practices did your group employ to manage, distribute, and execute on their work?

At the beginning of the semester, we spent a fair amount of time talking about our interests and specialties, which allowed us to decide on well-defined roles for people to focus in on. These were not limiting by any means, but it gave us a good sense of how to logically divide out the work and separate our program into actionable chunks. We used Discord for regular communication, held a shared Google Drive, posted tasks to our GitHub project page and updated them regularly, and did interactive code reviews via pull requests.

In your estimation, how well did your group follow through on using these processes throughout the semester?

In general, I think my group did quite well using these processes. We stayed in consistent contact with each other on Discord, which was instrumental in coordinating work, notifying each other of new PRs, and clarifying expectations. While most of the group was good about keeping the team updated, some were not as successful as I was hoping in terms of attendance and reaching out, making it hard to know what to expect from others. We also had similar issues with the GitHub project/issues page: most of us were using it regularly, but one member rarely interacted with it, so it was a hard to gauge their progress or set team goals. I think that splitting our responsibilities up like we did at the beginning of the semester was integral to being able to get a good start and have people develop simultaneously and asynchronously.

What, if anything, got in the way of integrating these process into your workflow?

I think we integrated most everything we wanted to without much difficulty. Most of us had to spend some time re-familiarizing ourselves with Git, so there were occasional mistakes in the more complicated actions. We had some small interpersonal issues around lack of communication, and at some point, we just learned to work with what we were given.
